THE PHILOSOPHY

Small systems.
Real momentum.

THE FRAMEWORK

Four rules to lock in

01 / CLARITY

Choose three priorities

One must-do, one important task, and one small win. Keep the rest in a later list.

02 / FOCUS

Work in one block

Pick one specific outcome and give it 25 focused minutes. Start smaller if needed.

03 / CONSISTENCY

Make it repeatable

Attach a new habit to an existing routine. Aim for progress you can sustain.

04 / REVIEW

Adjust, don't quit

At the end of the week, notice what worked and change one thing for next week.
